I work across product strategy, discovery and delivery. My job is to make sure the team builds the right thing, ships it well, and can show what it changed.

Problem Framing
Before anyone builds, I get the team clear on:

  • The customer and business outcome we're after
  • How the workflow and system work today, and their constraints
  • What success looks like, as a metric
  • Which bets we're making and which we're not

Discovery & Validation
I find and test answers fast by:

  • Running Jobs-to-Be-Done and journey mapping work with executives and customers
  • Mining support, usage and call center data, often with AI
  • Prototyping in Figma or with AI to test concepts early
  • Testing with real users before committing engineering time

Prioritization & Alignment
I keep product, design, engineering and leadership pointed the same way by:

  • Weighing options on impact, ROI and engineering feasibility
  • Writing project charters and requirements people can act on
  • Making decision rights and trade-offs explicit
  • Getting buy-in early from the people who carry the work

Delivery & Measurement
I stay with the work through release so that:

  • Scope stays tight enough to ship and learn
  • Edge cases and quality issues are caught before customers find them
  • Results are tracked against the success metric
  • Outcomes are reported back to stakeholders and executives

Leading Product Managers
At Melaleuca I lead a team of seven product managers and coach them at every level:

  • Junior PMs, hard skills: analysis, discovery, planning, presentations, cross-functional roles and metrics
  • Junior PMs, soft skills: communication, product judgment, applying agile philosophy, team dynamics and critical thinking
  • Mid-level PMs: product strategy, executive and business interaction, a strategic focus on outcomes, and general leadership

I'm most effective with ownership of outcomes, not just a backlog.
